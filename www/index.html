HOW TO PLAY CYBERFLUX

  • 1. Tap to Merge: Tap any tile connected to at least 1 other tile of the same level (up/down/left/right). All connected tiles collapse into the tapped spot and level up!
  • 2. Chain Reactions: As new tiles drop via gravity, if the newly upgraded tile matches neighbors, you can rapidly chain combos for massive score multipliers and bonus Flux Energy.
  • 3. Malware & Virus Nodes (👾): Every few moves, a corrupted Virus tile spawns. Viruses block normal merges—destroy them by merging a 4+ tile cluster next to them, or use EMP Blast / Firewall!
  • 4. Singularity Goal: Reach Lv.10 SINGULARITY to trigger an automatic board explosion and huge bonus points!
  • 5. Daily Blitz vs Endless Mode: Tap the ∞ / 📅 button at the top right to switch between Endless Overdrive and seeded Daily Tactical Blitz (35-move limit).
